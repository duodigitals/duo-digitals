A few helpful details about working with Duo Digitals. If you have a question that is not covered here, get in touch and tell us about your project.

What services does Duo Digitals offer?

We offer web development, web designing, content creation, content writing, graphic designing, UI/UX design, branding, digital marketing, SEO, social media management, video making and editing, and custom digital solutions.

How do I choose the right service for my business?

Start by telling us your goal or challenge. Whether you want to improve your website, build a recognizable brand, reach more people, or create better content, we can discuss the service or combination of services that best fits your needs.

How does the project process work?

We begin by understanding your goals and audience, then agree on a direction and priorities. Next, we create and refine the work with feedback, before preparing the agreed deliverables for handover or launch.

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on the type of service, project scope, complexity, and timeline. Share a few details about what you need, and we can discuss a suitable scope and quote.

How long will my project take?

Timelines vary depending on the service and the amount of work involved. Once we understand your requirements, we can discuss a realistic schedule and the information or feedback needed from you.

Can I request changes or revisions?

Yes. Feedback is part of a collaborative process. The number and type of revisions should be agreed before work begins so expectations are clear for everyone.

What should I provide to get started?

It helps to share your project goal, target audience, preferred style, timeline, and any existing brand materials or examples you like. If you are not sure yet, simply describe your idea and we can take it from there.

How can I get started with Duo Digitals?

Use the contact form below to tell us about your project and select the service you are interested in. We can then discuss the next steps and clarify any details.

Do you work with small businesses and startups?

Yes. We can discuss projects for startups, small businesses, and established teams. The right approach depends on your goals, budget, and the support you need.

Can I request more than one service?

Absolutely. If your project needs a website, branding, content, or marketing support, tell us how the pieces fit together. We can discuss a coordinated approach based on your priorities.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Responsive design is an important part of a modern website. Share your website requirements with us so we can confirm the scope and make mobile usability part of the plan.

Can you refresh my existing website or brand?

Yes, we can discuss refreshing an existing website or brand identity. Tell us what is working, what needs improvement, and what you want your updated presence to achieve.

Do I need to have a complete idea before contacting you?

No. A rough idea is a perfectly good starting point. Share what you are trying to achieve, and we can help clarify the requirements and possible next steps.

How will we communicate during the project?

We aim to keep communication clear and organized, with agreed check-ins and feedback points. The communication method and schedule can be confirmed before the project begins.

Will I own the final files and deliverables?

Ownership, file formats, and handover details should be agreed in the project scope. We will clarify which final files and deliverables are included before work starts.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Ongoing support depends on the service and what is agreed for your project. Tell us if you need maintenance, updates, or continued creative and digital support so we can discuss suitable options.

What if my requirements change during the project?

If your needs change, let us know as early as possible. We can review how the change affects the scope, timeline, and cost before agreeing on the next steps.

Can you work with my existing brand guidelines?

Yes. Share your brand guidelines, logos, colors, fonts, and any relevant materials at the start. We can use them to help keep the work consistent with your established identity.

How do I know which package or budget is right?

You do not need to choose a package before reaching out. Explain your goals, must-have features, and approximate budget, and we can discuss a realistic scope to consider.
